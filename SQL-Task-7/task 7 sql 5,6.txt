5. normalization into 2nf

unnormalized table (repeating groups)

order_id	customer_name	product1	product2
101		arun		laptop		mouse
102		Priya		mobile		null

2nf tables

customers

customer_id	customer_name
1		arun
2		priya

orders

order_id	customer_id
101		1
102		2

order_products

order_id	product_name
101		laptop
101		mouse
102		mobile

This removes repeating groups and ensures each non-key attribute depends on the whole key.

6. example of 3nf

table not in 3nf

student_id	student_name	dept_id	dept_name
1	        ravi		d01	cse
2	        priya		d02	ece

Here, dept_name depends on dept_id, not directly on student_id (transitive dependency).

3nf tables

students

student_id	student_name	dept_id
1	        ravi		d01
2	        priya		d02

departments

dept_id	dept_name
d01	cse
